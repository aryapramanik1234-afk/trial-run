A little about us

A table, a cup,
and time to spare.

Monsoon & Mug began with a simple thought: the best cups are the ones you have time to enjoy. We bring thoughtfully brewed coffee, warm pastries and easygoing plates together in a space that feels like your favourite corner of the neighbourhood.

Come in from the rain, catch up with a friend, or bring a book and stay a while. There’s always room for one more cup.
